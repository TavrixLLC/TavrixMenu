# Product Editor Asset Provenance

## Approved reference

[create-product-approved.png](create-product-approved.png) is the authoritative visual reference for the Create Product direction. It is not a production asset and must not be bundled into the application.

## Production constraints

- Do not crop, trace, extract, or ship icons, upload artwork, text, controls, backgrounds, or any other element from the screenshot.
- The production image area uses native Flutter UI and supported platform image selection.
- Merchant product images come from real merchant-selected uploads through the approved backend flow.
- No remote generated image URL may be hard-coded.
- Do not copy Stitch HTML, generated code, or Stitch asset bundles.
- Do not use Btaqa branding, photographs, icons, layouts, or other assets.
- No screenshot-derived production asset is allowed.
- Product placeholders require separately approved original assets with recorded rights or native Flutter composition.
- Future files under `variants/` are non-authoritative unless a later human review promotes an exact state explicitly.
