# Owner Dashboard Review Record

## Approved direction

The approved direction is the returning-owner, one-category, zero-product dashboard shown in [returning-zero-products-approved.png](returning-zero-products-approved.png). It establishes an Arabic-first RTL dashboard with a prominent next-step card, clear operational shortcuts, compact metrics, honest empty activity, loyalty education, and five equal bottom-navigation tabs.

## Known visual decisions

- Primary emphasis uses Waflo deep red as a normal action color, not as the semantic error color.
- The first-product task has the strongest hierarchy.
- Quick actions are secondary to the hero task.
- Unavailable notification behavior is visibly muted and explained.
- Metrics use compact cards and must represent real values or honest unavailability.
- The scanner is an equal bottom-navigation tab; it does not float or enlarge.
- The selected navigation treatment must later match Menu Management and every other shell screen.
- Illustrations are compositional direction only and may not be cropped from the approved raster.

## Variant status

Dashboard variants are non-authoritative explorations:

- `variants/owner-dashboard-v01.png`, `owner-dashboard-v04.png`, and `owner-dashboard-v05.png` explore alternate zero-product directions.
- `variants/owner-dashboard-v02.png` is a populated exploration.
- `variants/owner-dashboard-v03.png` introduces orders and sales behavior outside the currently approved Waflo scope.
- None of the variants may override the canonical approved visual, documented states, or product scope.

## Acceptance boundary

The direction is approved, but implementation has not started. Final implementation requires human visual QA at supported widths and on representative devices. Codex may identify mismatches but must not declare `HUMAN_VISUAL_PASS`.
