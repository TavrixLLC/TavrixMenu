- Do not crop, trace, extract, or ship illustrations, icons, text, backgrounds, or interface fragments from the screenshot.

- Do not use Btaqa assets, branding, text, layout, or proprietary visuals.
- Use licensed iconography or native platform/Flutter primitives with recorded provenance.
